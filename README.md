# The Corrosion of Guilt

A branching detective noir story with 16 unique endings.

## Overview

Follow P.I. Kael Rourke as he navigates a labyrinth of corruption, guilt, and moral compromise in pursuit of the serial killer known as "The Whisper." Every choice matters. Every decision has consequences.

## Structure

- **12 Chapters** organized into 5 phases
- **11 Decision Points** that branch the narrative
- **16 Unique Endings** based on your choices
- **Multiple Paths** tracking assets, liabilities, and moral decay

## Reading Guide

Start with Chapter 1 and make your choices at each decision point. Track your path through the story to discover one of sixteen possible endings.

- `story/` - Contains all chapters
- `story/outline.md` - Full branching structure and decision tree
